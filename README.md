# 3000won_stock.ai

## Introduction

**3000won_stock.ai** is an AI-powered investment assistant designed to help individual investors make more informed, data-driven decisions without requiring advanced financial knowledge.

The project analyzes historical stock market data, sector performance, ETF allocation trends, seasonal market behavior, and risk-adjusted returns to generate practical investment insights. Rather than attempting to predict the future with certainty, the AI focuses on identifying statistically favorable opportunities based on historical evidence and portfolio optimization techniques.

The goal is to provide affordable and accessible investment intelligence that can assist users with:

- Identifying historically strong-performing stocks during specific periods
- Discovering seasonal investment opportunities
- Optimizing ETF portfolio allocations
- Evaluating risk versus expected return
- Generating data-driven investment recommendations
- Supporting both long-term investors and short-term traders

---

## Key Features

### Historical Performance Analysis

Analyze multiple years of stock market data to identify patterns and recurring opportunities.

Examples:

- Best-performing stocks for a specific month
- Historical annual return rankings
- Sector rotation analysis
- Seasonal market trends

Example query:

> "Which U.S. stocks achieved the highest average return during July over the past 10 years?"

---

### Seasonal Investment Intelligence

The AI studies historical price movements to discover recurring seasonal effects.

Examples:

- Stocks that tend to perform strongly in specific months
- Industries that outperform during particular economic cycles
- Historical market behavior before earnings seasons

---

### ETF Portfolio Optimization

Evaluate and rebalance ETF allocations based on:

- Risk tolerance
- Investment objectives
- Historical volatility
- Correlation analysis
- Diversification efficiency

Example:

Current Portfolio:

- 60% S&P 500 ETF
- 20% Technology ETF
- 20% Bond ETF

The AI can suggest adjustments to improve diversification and risk-adjusted returns.

---

### Risk Analysis Engine

Measure and visualize:

- Volatility
- Maximum drawdown
- Sharpe Ratio
- Sortino Ratio
- Portfolio concentration risk

This helps investors understand potential downside exposure before making investment decisions.

---

### Scalp Trading Assistant

For short-term traders, the AI can analyze:

- Intraday volatility
- Volume spikes
- Momentum indicators
- Technical signals
- Relative strength metrics

The system highlights stocks that meet predefined trading conditions.

---

### AI Investment Recommendation System

Generate investment suggestions using a combination of:

- Historical performance data
- Technical indicators
- ETF allocation analysis
- Market trend analysis
- Risk assessment models

Recommendations are presented with supporting data and confidence scores rather than simple buy/sell signals.

## Project Vision

3000won_stock.ai aims to democratize quantitative investment analysis by providing affordable, transparent, and evidence-based financial insights.

Instead of relying on speculation, hype, or emotional decision-making, the platform encourages disciplined investing through historical analysis, portfolio optimization, and AI-assisted research.

The long-term objective is to build a personal AI investment analyst that can help investors understand market behavior, manage risk, and improve decision-making with data-driven intelligence.
